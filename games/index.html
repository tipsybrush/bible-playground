Free Bible games

Every game on Bible Playground, free to play on your phone or computer with no sign-up. Made for teens, young adults, youth groups and fellowships.

  • Bible QuizPlay a free Bible quiz online. Ten Bible trivia questions from the Old Testament, New Testament or both, easy to hard, with a global leaderboard.
  • Jacob's LadderClimb 15 rungs to a million points in this millionaire-style Bible quiz. Three safe zones, harder questions at the top and lifelines like Ask the Youth Group.
  • Story GapsFill in the blanks of famous Bible verses and Bible stories. A free Scripture memory game with a word bank, decoys and KJV passages.
  • Who Am I?Guess the Bible character from three clues. Free Bible riddles for teens and youth groups: the sooner you solve it, the more points you score.
  • Bible CharadesBible charades on your phone. A free pass-the-phone party game for youth group, fellowship and camp with Bible characters, stories and objects.
  • Word of the DayGuess the 5-letter Bible word in 6 tries. A free daily Bible word puzzle: everyone gets the same word, so compare results with friends.
  • Books of the Bible SnakeLearn the books of the Bible in order with a snake game. Eat Genesis to Malachi or Matthew to Revelation in the right order, fast.
  • Timeline RushA Bible timeline game: put Bible events in the order they happened before the clock runs out. From Creation to the early church.
  • Two by TwoA Noah's Ark memory game. Match the animals in pairs and load the ark before the flood rises. Free, quick and fun.
  • Bible Map DashA Bible geography game. Find Bethlehem, Jericho, Damascus and more on the map of Bible lands. The closer you tap, the more you score.
  • Bible CrosswordPlay a free Bible crossword puzzle online. A new daily Bible crossword for everyone plus unlimited practice puzzles, with hints and verse references.
  • Where Is It Written?Read a Bible verse and guess which book it comes from. A free Bible verse game with bonus points for the chapter and a streak mode.
  • Wilderness TrailLead Israel from Egypt to the Promised Land in this Exodus adventure game. Manage manna, water, morale and faith. Six endings to find.
  • Two Truths and a LieTwo truths and a lie about Bible characters. Spot the classic Bible mix-up fast. A free Bible game for teens, youth groups and small groups.
  • David's SlingSee the valley through David’s eyes in this David and Goliath game. Pull back the sling, mind the wind and aim fast.
  • Department WarsA team game for church youth fellowship and camp. Choir vs Ushers vs Media: one host, one phone or projector, a big scoreboard and Bible rounds.
  • Find Your PlaceA free 12-question quiz for young people: find what you are passionate about, where you could serve at church and which team can help you grow.
  • Which Bible Character Are You?Take the free Which Bible Character Are You quiz. Ten quick questions: are you a Peter, an Esther, a David or a Barnabas?
  • Kingdom Career MatchA Christian career quiz for students. Match what you love and what you are good at to your top 3 career paths, courses to study and a Bible person who did similar work.
  • Your Bible Journey TwinWaiting, starting over, feeling small or stepping up? Take the quiz and meet the Bible person whose journey looks like your season of life.

Privacy

Bible Playground is free and needs no sign-up. We look at how the games are played so we can make them better, and we keep that to the minimum.

What we collect

  • Usage counts. Which pages and games are opened, game scores and times, and when a score is shared. Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics turn this into totals for us, with your rough country and the kind of device you use.
  • Anonymous replays. Microsoft Clarity records clicks, taps and scrolling as anonymous replays and heatmaps, so we can spot games that confuse people. What you type into name boxes is hidden from these replays.
  • Leaderboards. If you play a scored game, your nickname, score and time go on the public leaderboard. Pick any nickname you like; you never need to use your real name.

What we never collect

  • No accounts, passwords, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No advertising: analytics is set up with ad personalisation and Google signals switched off, and nothing is sold.

Saved on your device

Your coins, level, nickname, settings and a random private player number are kept in your own browser so your progress is there next time. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Home page order

Drag the rows, or use the arrows on a phone. "Discover Yourself" is a row too, so you can move it anywhere. Hidden games disappear from the home page but their links still work. "New" puts a red NEW tag on the cover. Press Save to put it live; players see it on their next visit.

    Announcement banner

    A yellow line shown above the games, for news like a new game, a challenge weekend or an event. Keep it short.

    Leaderboards

    Remove a single score, or remove a player from every leaderboard if their nickname is rude or they cheated. This can't be undone.
